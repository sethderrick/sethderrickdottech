---
title: "The AI-bberwocky"
date: 2024-01-24
description: "An absurdist poem about coding in the age of AI"
tags: ["poetry", "ai", "coding"]
---

'Twas brillig in the Silicon Vale,
Did gyre and prompt the AI-loops all,
All mimsy were the neural scales,
And the deep nets did crawl.

"Beware the Copilot, my son!
The suggestions that bite, the completions that catch!
Beware the ChatGPT, and shun
The verbose StackOverflow dispatch!"

He took his keyboard bold in hand,
Long time the syntax foe he sought—
So rested he by the Git-Git tree
And stood awhile in thought.

And as in tech-deep thought he stood,
The Copilot, with eyes of flame,
Came whiffling through the coding wood,
And refactored as it came!

One-two! One-two! And through and through
The virtual blade went snicker-snack!
It left bugs dead, and with their head,
It went galumphing back.

"And hast thou slain the legacy code?
Come to my arms, my beamish boy!
O frabjous day! Callooh! Callay!"
He chortled in his joy.

'Twas brillig in the Silicon Vale,
Did gyre and prompt the AI-loops all,
All mimsy were the neural scales,
And the deep nets did crawl.
